ES-PET Mini 4C editable 200x200 storybook masters.

These source PNGs may contain ordinary RGB colours.  Run:

    python3 tools/generate_pet_art_4c.py

to create the strict 112x80 BLACK / WHITE / RED / YELLOW panel sprites and
regenerate src/pet_art.cpp.

The central 200x143 area of each master is the composition that becomes the
112x80 Mini art panel.  Keep important faces/objects inside that band.

factory_reset_alt.png is an optional alternate and is not currently embedded.
