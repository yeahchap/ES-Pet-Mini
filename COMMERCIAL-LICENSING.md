ES-Pet Mini is source available for non-commercial use under the public licences
listed in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md). Individuals can build and enjoy their own
Minis, modify the software and share permitted non-commercial adaptations while
following the applicable licence terms.

## Commercial products and services

If you plan to sell devices or kits containing ES-Pet Mini firmware or artwork,
include the project in a paid product or service, or otherwise use its protected
material commercially outside the public licences' permissions, obtain a separate
written commercial licence before doing so.

Commercial permission is available by individual agreement with **yeahchap** for
material the maintainer has the rights to license. Pricing may be a fixed fee,
a per-unit royalty or another mutually agreed arrangement. No price or royalty
rate is set by this notice.

## Make an enquiry

[Open a commercial licensing enquiry](https://github.com/yeahchap/ES-Pet-Mini/issues/new?title=Commercial%20licensing%20enquiry)
in this repository and outline:

- Your proposed product or service and how it would use ES-Pet Mini.
- Which code, artwork or documentation you want to use.
- The expected number of devices or users, and your intended launch timing.

An enquiry, acknowledgement, donation or discussion does not grant commercial
permission. The authorised use and any payment terms must be agreed in a separate
written commercial licence with the relevant rights holder.

## Existing permissions

The public licences remain available for the uses they permit. This includes the
educational, charitable and other organisational uses expressly permitted by
PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0. Applicable statutory exceptions also remain
unaffected. This page does not narrow those permissions.

Third-party components retain their own licence terms. A commercial licence from
yeahchap covers only rights that yeahchap can grant; it does not replace any
separate obligations for those components.
